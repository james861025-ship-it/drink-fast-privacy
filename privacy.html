Drink Fast · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-29

Drink Fast does not collect any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device and does not send any information to remote servers.

The only data stored locally on your device is app settings (vibration preference, premium unlock status). This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services

Drink Fast does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

This app contains drinking game content and is intended for adults 17 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 17.

5. In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (e.g., premium game modes) are processed by Apple's App Store. No payment information passes through our app.
