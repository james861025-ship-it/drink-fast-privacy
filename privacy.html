Drink Fast · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

1. Data Collection

Drink Fast does not collect any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device and does not send any information to remote servers.

The only data stored locally on your device is app settings (vibration preference, language choice, premium unlock status, and tip count). This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services

Drink Fast does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

4. Age Restriction

This app contains drinking game content and is intended for adults 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

5. In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (Tip Jar donations and premium game modes) are processed by Apple's App Store. No payment information passes through our app.
